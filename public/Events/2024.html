Events@Pi

The department always focuses on all-round development of the students, there’s a belief in the department that only studies can’t get you to the top, there are a lot of things which can be done along with studies for complete development of a student. In light of this, the department organizes a lot of events every year.

Pi-logue Competition '24

On March 14th, 2024, Pi: Department of Mathematics, Hansraj College commemorated International Pi Day with an event named Pi-Logue, celebrating the transcendental nature of pi and the wonders of mathematics. The event commenced with the traditional lighting of the lamp ceremony, symbolizing enlightenment and knowledge. Speaker Session: Distinguished mathematician Prof. Geetha Venkataraman, from Ambedkar University, Delhi, delivered an enlightening session on the multifaceted world of mathematics, emphasizing its diverse applications, projects, and research avenues, particularly at the undergraduate level. Prof. Venkataraman, a specialist in Group Theory, expounded upon various mathematical appliances and ongoing projects at the undergraduate level, highlighting the significance of undergraduate research journals in fostering mathematical inquiry. Her illustrious academic journey, from earning a bachelor's degree from St. Stephen’s College, University of Delhi, to receiving a doctoral degree from the University of Oxford, underscored her expertise and passion for the subject.

Identity '24

The Department of Mathematics at Hansraj College, University of Delhi, hosted its annual cultural fest "Identity" as part of the larger college fest "Confluence" on April 8, 2024. The event took place at LP, Hansraj College, starting at 11:30 AM. "Identity" offered a unique blend of intellectual excitement and fun, attracting students from across Delhi University. Whether participants were seasoned mathematicians or simply curious about the beauty of numbers, the fest promised an unforgettable experience filled with learning and inspiration. A variety of games were available to play, catering to different interests and skill levels: 1. Soak 'n Score 2. Holly Bolly 3. Ball Bonanza 4. Guess Sketch Galore 5. Minute Munch Madness 6. Bottle Topple Tic Tac Toe These games provided participants with opportunities to showcase their talents and luck while having a great time. Students, teachers, and mathematics enthusiasts alike actively engaged in the festivities, contributing to the vibrant atmosphere of the event.
