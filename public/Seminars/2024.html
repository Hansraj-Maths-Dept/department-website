Workshop on LinkedIn Blueprint and Personal Branding

In today's digital age, the significance of maintaining a strong professional presence on LinkedIn cannot be overstated. Recognizing the importance of leveraging LinkedIn for career growth and personal branding, Pi: Department of Mathematics, Hansraj College organized an insightful events aimed at empowering students and faculty members in this aspect. Workshop : "LinkedIn Blueprint and Personal Branding": The workshop, held on 9th February, 2024, provided attendees with essential strategies to optimize their LinkedIn profiles, expand their networks, and enhance their personal brands. With the guidance of our esteemed guest, Mr. Ashok Aggarwal, Researcher at Invest India and a seasoned LinkedIn Coach, participants gained valuable insights into leveraging LinkedIn effectively for career advancement. Interaction with Mr. Ashok Aggarwal: Mr. Ashok Aggarwal, currently, a scholar and an advancing speaker, guided and directed about the significance of LinkedIn in career growth and opportunities. In this workshop, held in the New Seminar Room at 1 p.m., Mr. Aggarwal further delved into the intricacies of LinkedIn Blueprint and personal branding. Attendees had the opportunity to learn from Mr. Aggarwal's expertise and engage in intuitive discussions regarding the topics covered. Agenda of Workshop: The events covered a comprehensive range of topics essential for maximizing one's presence on LinkedIn, including: 1. Why LinkedIn is crucial in today's digital age. 2. Basic profile creation guidelines. 3. Strategies for profile optimization to enhance visibility. 4. Techniques for networking and expanding one's professional connections. 5. Leveraging referrals for career advancement. 6. Utilizing LinkedIn for finding internship opportunities and enhancing professional experience.

Fermat, Euler, and Secure Communication

The Department of Mathematics at Hansraj College, in collaboration with IQAC, organized a special lecture titled "Fermat, Euler, and Secure Communication." The lecture aimed to explore the profound contributions of mathematicians Fermat and Euler to the field of cryptography and their impact on modern secure communication protocols. Speaker: Dr. K. Srinivas, a distinguished Professor from The Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai, India, led the seminar. With expertise in Analytic Number Theory, Algebraic Number Theory, and Cryptography, Dr. Srinivas provided valuable insights into the mathematical principles underlying secure communication. Content: Dr. Srinivas elucidated the intricate concepts of cryptography, including encoding, decoding, and secure communication protocols. He leveraged examples to simplify complex ideas, making the subject accessible to all attendees. Through the lens of Fermat and Euler's work, he showcased the elegance of mathematics in ensuring data confidentiality and integrity in the digital era. Acknowledgment and Honor: Following the lecture, Dr. Rama (Principal), Dr. Raksha Verma (Mathematics Professor) and the fellow professors honored Dr. Srinivas for his enlightening discourse. The Vice President expressed gratitude to all participants for their contributions to the success of the session.

Pi-Logue

On March 14th, 2024, Pi: Department of Mathematics, Hansraj College commemorated International Pi Day with an event named Pi-Logue, celebrating the transcendental nature of pi and the wonders of mathematics. The event commenced with the traditional lighting of the lamp ceremony, symbolizing enlightenment and knowledge. Speaker Session: Distinguished mathematician Prof. Geetha Venkataraman, from Ambedkar University, Delhi, delivered an enlightening session on the multifaceted world of mathematics, emphasizing its diverse applications, projects, and research avenues, particularly at the undergraduate level. Prof. Venkataraman, a specialist in Group Theory, expounded upon various mathematical appliances and ongoing projects at the undergraduate level, highlighting the significance of undergraduate research journals in fostering mathematical inquiry. Her illustrious academic journey, from earning a bachelor's degree from St. Stephen’s College, University of Delhi, to receiving a doctoral degree from the University of Oxford, underscored her expertise and passion for the subject.
